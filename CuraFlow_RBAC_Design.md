# CuraFlow Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Visibility Guide

CuraFlow operates on a multi-tenant, agentic architecture. Because autonomous AI agents make real-time decisions, human oversight is strictly gated by roles. This document defines what each role can see and what actions they can perform.

---

## 1. Platform Super Admin (`super_admin`)
The highest level of access. This role is not tied to a specific hospital organization but spans the entire platform.

**Visible Data & UI:**
- **Org Switcher:** Can view and select any hospital organization on the platform.
- **Command Center Dashboard:** Full access to all widgets (Beds, ER, OT, Staffing, Claims).
- **AgentOps & System Health:** Deep visibility into AI agent executions, LangGraph trajectories, token usage, and latency.
- **Simulation Engine:** Full access to the "What-If" slider components to test load on any hospital.

**Permissible Actions:**
- Provision new hospital organizations and `admin` accounts.
- View and override *any* pending AI approval in the system.
- Deploy, pause, or rollback AI agent pipelines globally.
- Access raw database metrics and system health logs.

---

## 2. Hospital Administrator (`admin`)
The highest level of access *within a specific hospital organization*. 

**Visible Data & UI:**
- **Command Center Dashboard:** Restricted to their specific organization's data.
- **Staffing & Roster Analytics:** Full visibility into online/standby/offline doctors and nurses.
- **Financials / Claims:** Visibility into pending, approved, and rejected insurance claims.
- **Simulation Engine:** Can simulate surges (e.g., +50 ER patients) to trigger AI capacity planning.

**Permissible Actions:**
- Approve or reject high-level workflow changes (e.g., opening a new overflow ward).
- Manage staff user accounts (create, edit, delete doctors/nurses within their org).
- Issue Natural Language commands to the AI Orchestrator for capacity planning.

---

## 3. ER Coordinator (`er_coordinator`)
Focused entirely on the Emergency Room flow and triage.

**Visible Data & UI:**
- **ER & Bed Widgets:** Real-time visibility into ER wait times, inbound ambulances, and ICU/Ward bed availability.
- **Orchestrator Canvas (Read-Only):** Can see the visual flow of how agents are routing patients out of the ER.
- *Hidden:* Financials, OT schedules, and platform system health.

**Permissible Actions:**
- Approve or reject agent-recommended bed allocations.
- Trigger "Surge Protocols" via the AI command bar.
- Manually override triage priority suggested by the triage agent.

---

## 4. Operation Theater Manager (`ot_manager`)
Focused on surgical scheduling and OT resource management.

**Visible Data & UI:**
- **OT Widget:** Real-time status of all Operation Theaters (In-Progress, Scheduled, Cleaning, Available).
- **Surgeon Availability:** Roster of available surgical staff.
- *Hidden:* ER inbound queues, Claims, platform system health.

**Permissible Actions:**
- Approve AI-recommended rescheduling of elective surgeries due to emergency trauma cases.
- Dispatch cleaning agents (cleaning staff workflows) to turn over rooms.

---

## 5. Doctor (`doctor`)
Focused on patient care and medical decision oversight.

**Visible Data & UI:**
- **Mobile Approvals UI (`/mobile`):** A frictionless, swipe-based interface displaying critical medical recommendations.
- **Patient Lists:** Visibility into their assigned patients and patients ready for discharge.
- *Hidden:* Hospital financials, global bed counts (unless directly relevant to their patient), system health.

**Permissible Actions:**
- **Swipe-to-Approve:** Approve or reject clinical plans generated by the `clinical_agent` (e.g., care plans, medication orders).
- Request AI assistance for differential diagnosis or lab summaries via natural language prompts.
- Authorize patient discharges (which triggers the downstream billing and bed-cleaning agents).

---

## 6. Nurse (`nurse`)
Focused on ward operations, vitals monitoring, and execution of care plans.

**Visible Data & UI:**
- **Mobile Approvals UI (`/mobile`):** Swipe-based interface for operational and care tasks.
- **Ward Status:** Visibility into the specific departments/wards they are assigned to.
- *Hidden:* Financials, platform-wide metrics, OT queues.

**Permissible Actions:**
- **Swipe-to-Approve:** Approve or reject operational tasks (e.g., confirm a patient has been moved to a new bed).
- Acknowledge AI alerts for deteriorating patient vitals.
- Trigger pharmacy restock agents when local ward supplies run low.
